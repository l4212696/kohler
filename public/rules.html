Survivor

Everyone at a table stakes the same amount. When the table fills, a set number of players are knocked out. Everyone else gets their own stake back plus an equal share of the knocked-out stakes.

Fee

Kohler takes 10% of the knocked-out stakes only. Survivors' own stakes are never touched. Any leftover cent from rounding goes to the fee.

Provably fair draw

  • Before a round starts we publish a hash of a secret server seed.
  • Every seat adds its own random seed when it sits.
  • After the draw the server seed is revealed so anyone can check the result. For each knock-out i: sha256(server : sha256(seat seeds joined by "|") : roundId : i), the first 13 hex characters as a number, mod the seats still in.
  • Every finished round has a verify view with all the inputs.

House seats

  • On public tables of 5 or more seats that haven't filled after about 30 seconds, Kohler may take up to 2 seats so the round can start.
  • These seats are always shown as Kohler with a House tag. They are never presented as real players.
  • A house seat stakes the same and has exactly the same odds as every other seat.
  • Kohler never sits at private tables.

Cancelled rounds

If a round can't run, every stake is refunded in full to the player's balance.

Casino games

  • Kohler Originals (Rocket, Mines, Coin Flip, slots and tables) are made in-house. Results are decided on our server when you play.
  • Every game shows its return to player (RTP), the long-run share of stakes paid back. Over time the house keeps the rest.
  • Rocket and Mines rounds left open are settled automatically.
  • Demo mode uses play money and never touches your balance.

Deposits & withdrawals

Deposits

  • Send Bitcoin only to your personal address. Other coins or networks are lost.
  • Your balance is credited in dollars at the live price once the payment has enough confirmations.
  • Very small deposits are held for review instead of credited straight away.

Withdrawals

  • Paid in Bitcoin to the address you give. Most go out within 30 minutes.
  • A fee of 1% plus the Bitcoin network fee applies. The withdraw screen shows the exact amount and the minimum before you confirm.
  • We email you when it's sent. If a payment can't be sent, the full amount goes back to your balance.
  • Bitcoin payments can't be reversed, so check the address carefully.

Shop orders

  • Orders are paid from your Kohler balance. A 1% service fee is shown before you pay.
  • Delivery details (code, login or instructions) appear on your Orders page right after you pay. If we have a working email for you, we send a copy.
  • The contact you give at checkout is used only to help with that order.
  • Problem with an order? Use Help and we'll sort it out.

Vault

  • Avatars, frames and win effects are cosmetic. They have no cash value, can't be withdrawn and don't change any odds.
  • Custom avatars are painted by AI from your description and only you can use them. Describe a made-up, family-friendly character: no real people, brands or other franchises, and nothing violent or adult.
  • Any image that fails to paint is refunded. An order that isn't painted within a day is refunded in full.
  • We may remove a custom avatar that breaks these rules.

Playing safely

  • Games are for people aged 18 and over.
  • Only play with money you can afford to lose.
  • You can set a daily limit or take a break from Play limits. Lowering a limit is instant; raising or removing one waits 24 hours.
